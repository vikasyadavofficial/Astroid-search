About this data

  • Source: NASA/JPL Small-Body Database, the standard catalogue of known asteroids and comets.
  • Orbits are predictions. They are fitted to telescope observations and come with an uncertainty, shown as “±”. More observations over a longer time means a better-known orbit.
  • Values are rounded to about three significant digits to keep things readable.
  • Missing values are not guessed. If a property has not been measured or estimated, the page says “Not available”.
  • “Potentially hazardous” is a label for orbit geometry and size. It does not mean an asteroid is expected to hit Earth.

Asteroid Explorer is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or JPL.
